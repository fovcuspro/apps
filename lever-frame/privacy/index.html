Lever Frame Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

In short: Lever Frame doesn't collect any personal data. Nothing you do in the game is sent to me. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

Who I am

Lever Frame is made by Ross Cowen, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. If you have any question about this policy or your data, email rosscowen@hotmail.com.

What stays on your device

To work, Lever Frame saves a few things on your iPhone or iPad:

  • your best score and stars for each level
  • your sound setting

These stay on your device. They aren't sent to me or anyone else, and they're removed if you delete the app.

No adverts, analytics or tracking

Lever Frame contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party code that collects data. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Lever Frame doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR) you have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. As Lever Frame doesn't collect any, I hold nothing about you from the game. If you have a concern, email me; you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the game ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that update is released.
