Common questions

How does the full game unlock work?
You can play the first three boxes, Ashby, Fenby and Hollin Cross for free. Tap any box after Hollin Cross on the box select and the game offers the Full Timetable (£1.99 in the UK, once). Buy it and every box is yours; you still open them one by one with a star on the box before. Your stars and best scores carry on as they were.
I bought the unlock but it's locked again.
On the box select, tap Restore (top right, next to Sound). Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought it with.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I start again from scratch?
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears your saved progress and scores on that device. Your unlock isn't lost: use Restore Purchase.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad running iOS 18 or later.
