Keeper's Light Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email rosscowen@hotmail.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a few days. It helps to mention your device and iOS version.

Common questions

How does the full game unlock work?
The Harbour Light and the Rock Light are free, and so is today's lighthouse every day. The Lighthouse Unlock (£1.99 in the UK, once) opens the other six lighthouses and the Long Winter. On the title screen tap Lighthouses, then any locked lighthouse, to buy it.
How do I play?
Touch and hold to stop the beam, keep holding while the marker runs out along it, then let go to throw the lifebuoy that far. Whoever it lands beside is hauled home. At night you only see people while the beam passes over them.
I bought the unlock but it's locked again.
Open the game, tap the gear icon on the title screen for Settings, and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought it with.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
My scores aren't showing on the leaderboard.
Leaderboards and achievements use Game Center. Check you're signed in to Game Center in the Settings app, then finish a run. Scores can take a minute to appear.
How do I start again from scratch?
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears your saved progress and scores on that device. Your unlock isn't lost: use Restore Purchase.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad running iOS 18 or later.
