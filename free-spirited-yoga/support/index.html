Free-Spirited Yoga Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email rosscowen@hotmail.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a few days. It helps to mention your device and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Free-Spirited Premium, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
I've subscribed but the classes are locked.
In the app, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you subscribed with. If that doesn't work, email me.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
A class won't play or keeps stopping.
Classes stream over the internet, so check you have a connection (Wi-Fi is best for the longer classes). Close the class and start it again. If it still won't play, email me the class name and your device.
Why do some routines have music but no voice?
Nadia is recording voice guidance pose by pose. Until a pose has its recording, routines play its video with music only, and the voice appears on its own once it's ready.
Is yoga safe for me?
If you are pregnant, injured, recovering from surgery or have a medical condition, check with your doctor first. Stop if anything hurts. The app offers general guidance, not medical advice.
How do I delete my data?
Email me from the address below and I'll delete your practice history and routines from the server. Deleting the app removes the copy on your device.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later.
