Health and safety

Yoga is gentle, but it is still exercise. If you are pregnant, injured, recovering from surgery or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before you begin. Move within what feels comfortable, and stop if anything hurts or if you feel dizzy or short of breath.

Free-Spirited Yoga offers general guidance for healthy adults. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it doesn't replace a doctor, physiotherapist or in-person teacher. Make sure you have enough clear space and a non-slip surface. You practise at your own risk.
