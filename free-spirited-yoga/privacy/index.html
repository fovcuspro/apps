Free-Spirited Yoga Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

In short: Free-Spirited Yoga doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number. To save your practice and the routines you build, it creates an anonymous account on the app's server. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Who I am

Free-Spirited Yoga is made by Ross Cowen, an independent developer in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the data described here. For any question about this policy or your data, email rosscowen@hotmail.com.

What is collected, and why

An anonymous account ID
The first time you open the app it creates a random account ID. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID. It lets the app keep your own records separate from everyone else's.
Your practice history
For each session: the class or routine name, when you started and finished, how long you practised, and whether you finished it. This shows your history and streak in the app.
Routines you build
The name you give a routine, the poses in it and how long each is held, so you can play it and keep it.
Subscription status
If you subscribe, Apple tells the app's server which plan you have, whether it is active, and when it renews or ends, together with a random code that links it to your anonymous account ID. This is how the app knows to unlock the classes. Apple handles the payment; I never see your card details, name or Apple ID.
Technical information
Like any website or app that connects to the internet, the services below see your device's IP address and basic request details when the app fetches classes. They use these to deliver the video and audio and to keep the service secure, and keep them only briefly.

The legal basis for using this data is that it is needed to provide the app you asked for (contract), and, for security logs, my legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

What stays on your device

Your settings (voice level, music and volume), reminders you set, and a copy of your history and routines are also kept on your iPhone or iPad so the app works without a connection. Reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Health

Free-Spirited Yoga doesn't ask about your health, medical conditions or body measurements, and doesn't read or write Apple Health data. Your practice history records only which classes you did and for how long.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the app's database and audio files, in London, United Kingdom.
  • Cloudflare streams the class videos. Its network is worldwide, so the request for a video may be handled outside the UK; Cloudflare is bound by UK and EU data protection transfer safeguards.
  • Apple handles purchases and subscriptions under Apple's Privacy Policy.

These companies act on my instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes. Nobody else receives it.

How long it is kept

Your practice history and routines are kept for as long as your account exists. Subscription records are kept for as long as needed for billing questions and the law (up to six years). If you delete the app, the copy on your device goes, but the server copy stays linked to an anonymous ID that nobody can connect to you; email me and I'll delete it.

No adverts, analytics or tracking

Free-Spirited Yoga contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party code that collects data. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Free-Spirited Yoga is not designed for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has used it, email me and I'll delete their data.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR) you can ask for a copy of your data, and ask for it to be corrected, deleted or moved, or object to how it's used. Because the account is anonymous, the easiest way is to email rosscowen@hotmail.com from the app's Support link, and I'll help you find your records. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app starts collecting anything new, this page will say so before that update is released.
