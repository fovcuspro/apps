Padfall Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

In short: Padfall doesn't collect any personal data. Nothing you do in the game is sent to me. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

Who I am

Padfall is made by Ross Cowen, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. If you have any question about this policy or your data, email rosscowen@hotmail.com.

What stays on your device

To work, Padfall saves a few things on your iPhone or iPad:

  • your best scores and daily-shaft scores
  • your progress, ore, workshop upgrades, earned pods, achievements and play statistics
  • your settings

These stay on your device. They aren't sent to me or anyone else, and they're removed if you delete the app.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Padfall sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under your Game Center profile; I don't receive your details. Other players can see your Game Center nickname and scores depending on your Game Center settings. You can sign out of Game Center, or change who can see you, in the Settings app. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Full Game Unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; I never see your card details, name or Apple ID. The App Store only tells the game whether the unlock has been bought, so it can open the full game.

Sharing and ratings

If you tap Share on a result, the game makes a picture of your score and opens the iOS share sheet. It only goes where you choose to send it.

Now and then the game may ask you to rate it. That prompt is shown by Apple, and any rating goes to the App Store.

No adverts, analytics or tracking

Padfall contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party code that collects data. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Padfall doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR) you have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. As Padfall doesn't collect any, I hold nothing about you from the game. If you have a concern, email me; you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the game ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that update is released.
